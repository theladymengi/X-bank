9. Why are /etc/passwd and /etc/shadow separate files?

/etc/passwd stores basic user information that everyone can read, while /etc/shadow stores encrypted passwords that only administrators can access. This helps keep user passwords secure.

Why does the bank lock departed customers instead of deleting them?
Locking the account prevents login while keeping the user's files and records. This preserves important information for audits and future reference.

Why does the bank mandate sudo instead of sharing the root password?
sudo lets each manager use their own password to perform admin tasks. This improves security and keeps a record of who ran each command.
